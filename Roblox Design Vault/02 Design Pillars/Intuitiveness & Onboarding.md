# 🧩 Intuitiveness & Onboarding

> [!summary]
> Roblox players decide in **under a minute**. The top games win that minute with **zero-text teaching**: the title names the verb, the thumbnail shows it, the first object you see invites it, and the UI only shows what you need right now.

## The 5-point intuitiveness rubric

Score each criterion 1–5 (used in every game note):

| Criterion | 5 looks like… | 1 looks like… |
|---|---|---|
| **First 60 s clarity** | The first action is obvious without reading (a free carrot and a meter). | A tutorial wall of text. |
| **Verb count** | 1–3 verbs to play. | 8 systems on day one. |
| **Phone readability** | Big outlined numbers, ≥ 48 px targets, nothing under thumbs. | Tiny text, buttons in the thumbstick zone. |
| **Feedback** | Every action gets a sound, a pop, a number. | Silent state changes. |
| **Menu depth** | Everything ≤ 1 tap from the HUD; badges point the way. | Nested menus, unlabeled icons. |

🧠 **Pattern**: the newest chart-toppers score highest. Deep games (Blox Fruits, Forsaken) survive on depth and community instead — but a *new* game can't rely on that.

## Zero-text teaching techniques (seen in the top 12)

1. **The title is the tutorial** — *Throw Food For Animals*, *Steal An Egg*, *Fish It!*, *99 Nights in the Forest*.
2. **Emoji how-to in the store description** (✅ Steal An Egg, Throw Food For Animals, Steal a Brainrot, Survive Your Ideas all use "How to Play:" + emoji verb lines).
3. **The first reward is free and adjacent**: free carrot (📰 TFFA), first egg near spawn, starter units on the conveyor.
4. **Diegetic affordances**: duel pads (RIVALS), pull-up bars (TFFA), treadmills (Steal An Egg), conveyors (Brainrot) — objects that explain themselves.
5. **ProximityPrompts with verb + object** ("Steal · Yeti Egg") — native, consistent, accessible on every input.
6. **Arcs, rings, arrows** — show *where* before the player commits (TFFA throw arc, quest arrows).
7. **Badges and glows** point to the next menu.
8. **Lock, don't hide**: future zones are visible and walkable but gated by a number.

## Onboarding checklist

- [ ] Player performs the core verb within **10 seconds** of spawning.
- [ ] First reward within **30 seconds**; first upgrade within **2 minutes**.
- [ ] No text box longer than **one line** in the first 5 minutes.
- [ ] Every persistent HUD element has been **used** by minute 5.
- [ ] New-player protection where PvP exists (lock/shield for the first N minutes).
- [ ] The **like / favorite / group** ask comes *after* the first win, with a tangible reward (📰 Steal An Egg: 10,000 Speed).
