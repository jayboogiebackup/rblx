# 🖼️ Thumbnails & Icons

> [!summary]
> Before anyone sees your HUD, they see a **512×512 icon, a 16:9 thumbnail and a title** — usually tiny, on a phone. Every top game's store art follows the same grammar: **one verb, one hero object, one threat or reward, huge readable text, a 'YOU' marker, saturated colour, and a series template for A/B tests.**

🔎 **Icon patterns**: a single creature or character filling the frame, mouth open or mid-action (T-Rex biting meat, monster deer screaming, fishing a dragon), almost never a logo alone; strong rim light/outline against a simple background.

## Title patterns (✅ live chart)

- **Emoji + bracket tags** carry update news in the title: `[🎃] Blox Fruits`, `[UPD 🎃] Forsaken`, `[🌙UPD] Fish It! 🐟`, `[⚡] Adopt Me!`, `[💀] Steal a Brainrot`, `🎭 [MINIGAMES] Pet Simulator 99! 🎃`. See [[Live Chart Snapshot 2026-11-12]].
- **Verb-first titles** for new games: *Steal An Egg*, *Throw Food For Animals*, *Sweep The Moon*, *Survive Your Ideas*, *Break and Steal an Egg*.
- **Format names** that ride a trend on purpose ("Steal a ___", "Grow a ___", "___ Tower Defense").

## Checklist for your own store page

- [ ] Icon reads at **64 px**: one subject, one action, outline, no small text.
- [ ] Thumbnail shows the **verb mid-action** + the **prize** + a **threat or number**.
- [ ] Add a **'YOU!' arrow** or obvious player character (🔎 Steal An Egg).
- [ ] Put **big outlined numbers** (income/s, odds) — they sell progression.
- [ ] Use a **series template** (same composition, one variable swapped) and A/B test thumbnails.
- [ ] Title: **verb-first**, add an **update tag** when you ship (`[UPD]`, emoji).
- [ ] Description: **"How to Play:" + 6–8 emoji lines**, platforms supported, like/favorite ask with a reward.
- [ ] Refresh art for every event (Halloween 🎃, winter ❄️).
