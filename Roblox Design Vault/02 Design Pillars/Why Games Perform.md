# 🚀 Why Games Perform

> [!summary] The pattern across the top 12
> Top games combine **(1) a loop players already understand**, **(2) a visible number that grows**, **(3) social pressure** (theft, duels, trading, co-op), **(4) appointment reasons to return** (timers, events, drops), **(5) store art that sells one verb**, and **(6) frictionless discovery asks** (like / favorite / group / notify). Everything else is execution speed.

## The six performance drivers

### Loop fusion
The two newest #1-tier hits are fusions, not inventions. [[Steal An Egg]] = steal-tycoon + egg hatching + speed training. [[Throw Food For Animals]] = distance simulator + pen income + food-tier collection. ✅ *Fish It* openly credits "Fisch and Fishing Simulator"; ✅ *Break and Steal an Egg* credits "steal an egg"; ✅ *Sweep The Moon* credits "Moon Sweepers on Steam". **Iterate on proven loops; change one verb.**

### A number that visibly grows
Every simulator puts **$ per second** on screen and on the thumbnail (🔎 '$997,151,167/s', '$68,543B/S'). FPS and horror use streaks, day counters, levels.

### Social pressure
Theft (Steal An Egg, Brainrot), duels (RIVALS), trading (Adopt Me, MM2, PS99), co-op (99 Nights), roleplay (Brookhaven). ✅ Theft games run **tiny servers (5–8)** so rivals are personal; social games run **30–40**.

### Appointment mechanics
Egg respawns (📰 ~4.5 min), hourly fruit spawns (✅ Blox Fruits), 4-hour restocks (✅), hatch timers (✅), weekly updates, **admin-abuse events** (📰 the Aug 2025 Brainrot vs Grow a Garden event pushed Roblox to ~47.3M concurrent users).

### Store art that sells one verb
See [[Thumbnails & Icons]]. Verb-first titles, 'YOU!' markers, outlined numbers, series templates, emoji update tags in titles.

### Discovery asks with rewards
✅ "Like and join the group for exclusive rewards" (RIVALS), ✅ "Like & ⭐ Favorite to get new words first!" (Survive Your Ideas), ✅ "press 🔔NOTIFY" (Adopt Me), 📰 10,000 Speed for like + favorite + group (Steal An Egg). Likes, favorites and return visits are the signals discovery runs on.

## Anti-patterns (what the top 12 avoid)

- Pay-to-win in competitive modes (RIVALS stresses fair play & anti-cheat).
- Text-heavy tutorials.
- Hiding the exit or the price.
- Instant, unrecoverable PvP loss for new players.
- Shipping without an update tag / event cadence.
