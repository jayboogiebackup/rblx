## What the numbers say

- **Simulators run hot and bright**: [[Steal An Egg]], [[Throw Food For Animals]], [[Steal a Brainrot]], [[Fish It]] — high-saturation sky blue + grass green + one 'loot' accent (gold, purple). Nothing below ~#1B2028 except outlines.
- **[[RIVALS]] is a two-colour brand**: ~14% pure red `#FA0204` on ~38% white/lavender. The art direction *is* the readability rule (red target on pale arena).
- **Horror is mostly black**: [[99 Nights in the Forest]] 27% `#0B0C08`, [[Forsaken]] 29% `#0B050A`, [[Murder Mystery 2]] 22% `#000000` — the accent (fire orange, blood red, violet) does all the work.
- **[[Brookhaven RP]] is the most desaturated** (greys `#D6D6D5`, `#B9B8B8`): a believable suburb, so avatars and cars supply the colour.
- **[[Blox Fruits]] uses painted anime key-art** (navy + fire orange) — the only top game whose store images are illustrations rather than in-engine renders.
